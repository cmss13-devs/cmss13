Proc Details

proc
generate_water_overlay
(/list/input_icons, water_type, input_depth)

Water turfs do not have built in overlays anymore. They are now added directly on mobs upon entering these water turfs, handled by the component: /datum/component/water_overlay_effect Its unfesible to generate an overlay everytime a mob enters water, instead each water turf calles this proc to generate them all when all the atoms are first initialized.. and the results are added into a list in this subsystem to be fetched by the water_overlay components when needed: SSwater_overlays.water_overlay_icons

Every water turf uses a var water_type which is necessary because of water turfs like coasts, using their icon would add sand/dirt/grass into the water overlay texture Since every water turf is calling this proc on their init, we use generated_water_overlays and their depth and water_type as a key; determining if its already ran or not

Not every mob gets the same overlay, theres about 6 different sprite sizes mobs have, and lots behave differently in water and so need additional/different overlays.. e.g. humans resting in water So we use datum/water_overlay_config to inform each water turf what overlays we need (every mob ought to have one, or they ought not get a water overlay) for each config we check if the turf's depth applies and generate an overlay accordingly (some mobs ignore water while resting as some depths, or only use one overlay regardless of depth)

we use |= when adding overlays to eliminate duplicates, and since this proc is only ran once after the water turfs are initialized.. not to mention all congfigs complicating things coding checks for duplicates before running the logic seems not really worth the investment

ADDITIONAL NOTES: Because coasts generate overlays based off their water_type and because many coasts share the same depth, instead of having 8 or 12 overlays generated for every coast type we basically only generate 3(one for each coast depth) @param input_icons should be a list of all the different icons the water turf uses (get_all_possible_icon_files) @param water_type should be the water_type var all water turfs have @param input_depth should be the depth var all water turfs have

proc
update_turfs_layers_near_water
(/turf/water_turf)

update_turfs_layers_near_water() Goes through the given water turf's neighbors and adjusts layering on adjacent non-water turfs. We lower mob layers while in water so they render under turfs to their south (making them look submerged under the bank). For the northern shore this would incorrectly make tall mobs standing on land clip under the water, so those land turfs (and the ones flanking them) are pushed further down instead. Turfs already sit below everything else visually, so the only effect is preventing the unwanted clip.
