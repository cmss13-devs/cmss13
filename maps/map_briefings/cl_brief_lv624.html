Special Services Division
Liaison Colony Briefing

The ship AI aboard your stationed ship has informed us that a distress signal has been picked up from the colony on LV-624, and that a mission is being prepared to investigate the disturbance.

As such, the special services division has prepared a briefing on the nature of the colony, and known potential threats to it's operation.

Classified materials are excluded from this briefing and should be requested separately, and as such, the contents of this document may be shared or withheld at your discretion, unless otherwise specified.

Planet: LV-624
Facility: Lazarus Landing
Operator: Weyland-Yutani

History and Purpose

Discovered in 2068, the planet Kynagidas, also known as LV-624, was marked as a suitable environment for colonial settlement, requiring minimal terraforming for human habitation. Upon further investigation, the planet was found to be home to a variety of unique wildlife with value to The Company.

Weyland-Yutani claimed the colonial rights over the planet and set up several research sites to investigate the local flora and fauna. To prevent foreign contamination from alien life, a blockade made from the joint efforts of Weyland-Yutani and the Office of Colonial Marshals has limited access to the planet.

Lazarus Landing, the colony in distress, was founded as an archeological research site. With a population of 73, the colony employs a combination of Company workers, as well as small detachment of CMB deputies working with Weyland-Yutani to provide security for the colony.

The northern areas of the colony are used for shipping, cargo, mining operations, and archeological study. These areas are restricted to most colonists.

Company Priorities

The number one priority is that the archaeological site to the north remains undamaged. Violent conflict or the use of tools such as explosives should be avoided in this area. Any destruction or defacement of the site including theft of artifacts, damage, defacement, et cetera should be recorded and cited to the Company.

The second priority is to avoid contamination of wildlife. Chemical weapons are to be avoided wherever possible. No native wildlife should be brought onboard responding vessels as per the Pang Ho Treaty of '63. Any breach of this policy should be recorded. Information regarding said breach, including the offendor's name and rank, should be forwarded to the Colonial Marshal Bureau.

The Cargo sites within the colony contain valuable excavated ore. The retrieval or preservation of those areas is advised.

Maintain colonial security. The northern regions of the colony are off-limits to unauthorized colonists. Interview recovered personnel to ensure compliance with company standards. A failure to comply should be recorded and noted to The Company.

Potential Threats

The research colony contains only a token Colonial Marshals Bureau & Weyland security detachment, as to be expected of a colony of this size. The orbital blockade and small size of the facility has ruled out possible foreign actors. Data analysts believe these are the most likely causes for the automated distress beacon, from most to least likely.:

* Turbulent Weather Conditions; e.g. a tropical storm hitting the colony.
* Maintenance failure of the colony's communications equipment.
* Failure to send the weekly "All-Clear" signal to prevent automated distress being dispatched.
* Containment failure of wildlife research facility.
* Geological instability from archeological site
* Sabotage by the "Colonial Liberation Front"
* Orbital debris from LV-624's moon, RF/136.
