Commander,

New Varadero's Latin American Colonial Navy (LACN) Naval Base, has gone dark.

Originally founded by a joint Cuban-Australian collaborative effort on behalf of the dependencies' governments. Unrest in 2175 and LACN peacekeeping operations, have left the planet as a UA unincorporated colony.

Base targeting systems have confirmed that an unidentified craft of most likely of extraterrestrial origin crash-landed off the base's costal perimeter roughly four days ago.

Contact with the base's garrison was lost shortly thereafter, your mission is to assess the situation and restore control.
PRIMARY OBJECTIVES
  • - Establish Forward Operating Base nearby tunnel entrance to the naval base.
  • - Secure the crash site and assess the nature and threat of the vessel. Hostile presence is likely.
  • - Locate the LACN garrison and render aid.

SECONDARY OBJECTIVES
  • - Prevent looting or tampering with alien tech by USCM personnel.
  • - Isolate the vessel from power or signal transmission pathways.
  • - Eliminate any confirmed hostiles be they xenoform or otherwise.

Expect resistance. Expect irregularities.
Use your best judgment in an unconventional AO.
